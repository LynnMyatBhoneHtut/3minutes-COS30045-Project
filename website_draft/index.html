Beyond the Headlines

Road crash hospitalisations in Australia, 2011–2021. Team 3 minutes, COS30045.

Q1: How have hospitalisations changed?

They rose from 34,033 in 2011 to a peak in 2019, dropped in 2020 (COVID-19), then recovered.

Q2: Which road users are growing fastest?

Each line starts at 100 in 2011. Cyclists grew the most (+51%); pedestrians fell (−15%).

Read the data carefully

  • Counts, not rates: bigger states have more cases.
  • Series breaks: Victoria (2012) and NSW (2017) changed how they count.
  • Bed days were counted differently from 2015–16.
